# Moving day

The keys arrive on Friday. This note says what happens over the weekend and who does what, so that the office opens on Monday without surprises.

## Before the move

1. Pack your desk into the crates marked with your name.
2. Leave laptops and monitors on the desk, cables attached.
3. Check the floor plan for your new desk number.

## On the day

Teams move one zone at a time, starting at eight. The lifts are reserved for the movers until noon, so please use the stairs.

1. Zone A moves at eight.
2. Zone B moves at ten.
3. Zone C moves after lunch.

## Questions

Write to the operations mailbox at any time, or ask at the open session on Thursday at four.
